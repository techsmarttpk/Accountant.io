# Standard Deduction and Section 87A Rebate

The standard deduction is a flat deduction from salary income available
to salaried employees and pensioners, with no bills or proof required.
Under the new regime it is ₹75,000 for FY 2024-25 (raised from ₹50,000 in
the FY 2024-25 Union Budget); under the old regime it remains ₹50,000.

The Section 87A rebate makes tax payable effectively zero for taxpayers
below a certain taxable income threshold. Under the new regime for
FY 2024-25, the rebate fully cancels out tax liability (up to a maximum
rebate of ₹25,000) for taxable income up to ₹7,00,000. Under the old
regime, the rebate is smaller — up to ₹12,500 — for taxable income up to
₹5,00,000. Above these thresholds, tax is computed normally on the full
taxable income with no partial rebate.
