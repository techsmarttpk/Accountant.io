# Filing Basics: Form 16, ITR Forms, and Deadlines

Form 16 is a certificate issued by an employer summarizing salary paid
and tax deducted at source (TDS) during the financial year. It has two
parts: Part A shows TDS deposited with the government, and Part B shows
a detailed salary breakup, deductions claimed, and taxable income as
computed by the employer.

Most salaried individuals with no business or capital gains income file
ITR-1 (Sahaj). Those with capital gains, multiple house properties, or
foreign assets typically need ITR-2 or higher. The typical due date for
individuals not requiring an audit is July 31 following the end of the
financial year (which runs April 1 to March 31), though this date is
sometimes extended by the tax department — always confirm the current
year's deadline rather than assuming July 31 applies unchanged.

Filing after the deadline but before December 31 is still possible as a
belated return, subject to a late fee under Section 234F (₹1,000 for
income up to ₹5 lakh, ₹5,000 above that) and loss of certain benefits
like carrying forward capital losses.
