# Health and Education Cess, and Surcharge

A Health and Education Cess of 4% is levied on the income tax amount
(after any rebate) under both the old and new regimes. It is not a
separate slab-based tax — it is calculated as 4% of the base tax figure
and added on top.

A surcharge additionally applies to higher earners: 10% of tax if total
income exceeds ₹50 lakh, 15% if it exceeds ₹1 crore, 25% if it exceeds
₹2 crore, and 37% under the old regime (capped at 25% under the new
regime) if it exceeds ₹5 crore. The 4% cess is then calculated on the
tax amount inclusive of surcharge. Surcharge only applies to high-income
taxpayers and is not relevant for most salaried individuals.
