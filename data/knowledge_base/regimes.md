# Old Regime vs New Regime

India offers two parallel income tax regimes for individuals. The old
regime allows a wide range of exemptions and deductions (Section 80C,
80D, HRA, home loan interest under Section 24(b), etc.) but taxes income
at higher slab rates. The new regime (the default since FY 2023-24) uses
lower slab rates and a wider zero-tax bracket, but disallows most
deductions and exemptions — only the standard deduction and a small set
of exceptions (like employer NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2))
are still allowed.

Taxpayers with significant Section 80C investments, home loan interest,
or HRA claims often save more tax under the old regime. Taxpayers with
few deductions, or whose deductions don't add up to much relative to
their income, usually pay less under the new regime. There is no single
right answer — it depends on how large your actual deductions are
relative to your income, and should be computed both ways before filing.
