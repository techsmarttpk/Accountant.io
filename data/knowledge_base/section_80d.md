# Section 80D — Health Insurance Premiums

Section 80D allows a deduction for health insurance premiums paid,
available only under the old tax regime. An individual can claim up to
₹25,000 per year for premiums paid for self, spouse, and dependent
children. An additional ₹25,000 can be claimed for premiums paid for
parents, rising to ₹50,000 if the parents are senior citizens (age 60 or
above). If the taxpayer themselves is a senior citizen, their own limit
also rises to ₹50,000. This means a taxpayer with senior-citizen parents
could claim up to ₹75,000-₹1,00,000 combined under Section 80D depending
on their own age.

A smaller sub-limit of ₹5,000 (within the overall cap) is available for
preventive health check-up expenses.
