# HRA Exemption (Section 10(13A))

House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption is available only under the old tax
regime, and only to salaried employees who actually pay rent and receive
HRA as part of their salary. The exempt amount is the LEAST of the
following three figures:

1. Actual HRA received from the employer.
2. Rent paid minus 10% of (basic salary + dearness allowance).
3. 50% of basic salary if living in a metro city (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata,
   Chennai), or 40% of basic salary for a non-metro city.

Whichever of these three amounts is smallest is the portion of HRA that
is exempt from tax; the remainder is added to taxable salary. Rent
receipts and, for annual rent above ₹1,00,000, the landlord's PAN are
typically required to support an HRA claim.

Note: rent paid is not, by itself, a direct tax deduction — it only
matters for computing the HRA exemption via this formula, or (for the
self-employed / those without HRA in salary) under Section 80GG subject
to separate conditions and a lower cap.
